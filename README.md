## About Me

Through academic and project-based experiences, I have developed the ability to **analyze technical requirements, design practical solutions,** and **deliver results in collaborative environments.**

My hands-on experience includes **web application development, Internet of Things (IoT) systems, data analytics, UI/UX design,** and **information security assessments.** These projects strengthened my **problem-solving skills, technical adaptability,** and **attention to detail while ensuring project objectives and timelines are achieved.**

- **Leadership & Coordination:** Actively involved in leadership and organizational roles, contributing to team coordination, stakeholder communication, and successful project execution.
